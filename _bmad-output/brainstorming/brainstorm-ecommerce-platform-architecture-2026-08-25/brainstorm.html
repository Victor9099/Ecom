Domain Pressure Map

Stress load, failure, and ownership disputes until robust boundaries show themselves.

Product

Canonical identity, attributes, merges, splits, and supersession. Never seller price or stock.

Offer

Seller-specific price, condition, availability, and SLA.

Pricing

Versioned quotes with deterministic explanation trees.

Cart

Revocable intent; soft references only.

Order

The immutable commercial agreement: snapshot product, seller, price, tax, promise, and policy at purchase time.

Inventory

Stock positions, reservations, adjustments, and the no-oversell invariant.

Payment

Gateway attempts, authorization, routing, and retries.

Financial Ledger

Fulfillment

Disposable, reconstructible.

KYC, stores, staff, selling permissions.

05 / 05

Commerce Counterfactuals

Invert the foundational assumptions. The neglected models appear in the doorway.

Inventory is unknowable.

Preorder, made-to-order, and dropship need promise confidence—not binary availability.

Products are not physical.

Fulfillment becomes delivery of value: shipment, entitlement, subscription, booking, or service.

Price is negotiated later.

Quote becomes a durable versioned aggregate with expiry and explicit acceptance.

Payment arrives first.

Bank transfer reconciliation reveals the suspense account and claim workflow.

The customer is an organization.

Buyers, approvers, budgets, purchase orders, and tax exemptions enter without rewriting identity.

AI operates the back office.

Every action needs typed commands, previews, policy checks, idempotency, and reversible compensation.

SYNTHESIS

The architecture spine

Five lenses converge on one design: a commerce kernel plus replaceable capability modules, held together by public contracts—not shared internals.

OwnershipSchema per module

Each externally visible identifier and business invariant has one home.

InteractionPorts + adapters

Internal commands and queries can later cross a network without changing callers.

PropagationTransactional outbox

Domain events cross seams; consumers are idempotent and projections reconstructible.

GovernanceDependency fitness tests

Forbidden coupling fails the build; event compatibility becomes a gate.

ConsistencyExplicit islands

Order, inventory, and money keep the models their truths actually require.

EvolutionMaturity paths + trigger cards

Every capability knows how it may grow and what evidence justifies extraction.

EconomicsExtraction scorecards

Clean API, isolated data, event contracts, tests, projections, and a reversal window.
