- (idea by coach) Extract Payment when PCI scope isolation, provider reliability, or regional routing demands a separate security and availability boundary.
- (idea by coach) Extract Inventory when reservation contention breaches latency SLOs or stock must synchronize across multiple sales channels.
- (idea by coach) Extract Pricing when rule evaluation needs independent horizontal scale or business users deploy rules more often than application releases.
- (idea by coach) Extract Checkout when long-running retries and compensations require durable orchestration beyond the monolith process lifecycle.
- (idea by coach) Extract Media when image transformation, storage bandwidth, or CDN workflows dominate web-node capacity.
- (idea by coach) Extract Notification when provider throttling and delivery retries threaten transactional workload stability.
- (idea by coach) Extract Logistics when carrier integrations release independently and webhook bursts create distinct scaling behavior.
- (idea by coach) Extract Recommendation only after event volume and measurable conversion lift justify specialized models and infrastructure.
- (idea by coach) Extract Fraud when model inference latency, data isolation, or 24/7 risk-team deployment cadence diverges from core commerce.
- (idea by coach) Extract Merchant onboarding when KYC vendors, document handling, and regulatory workflows require stricter data boundaries.
- (idea by coach) Extract Settlement when payout windows, reconciliation batches, and financial controls need independent failure containment.
- (idea by coach) Do not extract Order merely because it is central; extract only when team ownership, throughput, or regional partitioning provides measurable benefit.

- (idea by coach) Use an internal gateway interface from day one so in-process calls can later route over transport without changing callers.
- (idea by coach) Place an outbox at every future extraction seam and require consumers to be idempotent before network distribution.

- (technique by coach) started Commerce Counterfactuals â€” invert foundational assumptions to reveal neglected models and differentiators
- (idea by coach) Assume there is no single checkout: each seller can negotiate fulfillment and payment terms, suggesting a reusable Deal or Contract domain.
- (idea by coach) Assume products are not physical: entitlements, subscriptions, bookings, and downloads reveal Fulfillment as delivery of value, not shipment.
- (idea by coach) Assume inventory is unknowable: preorder, made-to-order, and dropship flows need promise confidence rather than binary availability.
- (idea by coach) Assume price is negotiated after cart creation: Quote must be a durable, versioned aggregate with expiry and acceptance.
- (idea by coach) Assume payment arrives before the order: bank transfer reconciliation needs a suspense account and claim workflow.
- (idea by coach) Assume one purchase creates ten sellers and five shipments: model an Order Group with seller Orders and fulfillment Packages.
- (idea by coach) Assume the customer is an organization: introduce accounts, buyers, approvers, budgets, purchase orders, and tax exemptions without rewriting identity.
- (idea by coach) Assume the seller is also a warehouse: merchant locations become fulfillment nodes with explicit capabilities and calendars.
- (idea by coach) Assume returns never travel back: disposition can be keep, donate, destroy, repair, or route to a different node.
- (idea by coach) Assume promotions are liabilities: issue benefits through a value ledger so gift cards, store credit, loyalty, and coupons share accounting rigor.
- (idea by coach) Assume the storefront is only one client: expose channel-neutral experience APIs for web, mobile, POS, social, and partner marketplaces.
- (idea by coach) Assume connectivity disappears: seller operations need offline-capable task queues and conflict-resolution semantics.
- (idea by coach) Assume regulations conflict by region: tenant and legal-entity boundaries must be separable from brand and storefront boundaries.
- (idea by coach) Assume a product changes identity: model merges, splits, supersession, and redirects rather than treating SKU as eternal.
- (idea by coach) Assume the platform sells services alongside goods: scheduling and capacity inventory become peers of stock inventory.
- (idea by coach) Assume every recommendation must be explainable: retain feature provenance and customer-control signals in personalization.
- (idea by coach) Assume seller failure is common: escrow-like holds, fallback fulfillment, order reassignment, and customer protection become explicit capabilities.
- (idea by coach) Assume partial success is normal: every multi-line operation exposes per-item outcomes instead of a misleading global status.
- (idea by coach) Assume time is a first-class dimension: promises, reservations, prices, policies, and permissions all carry effective and expiry intervals.
- (idea by coach) Assume AI agents operate the back office: every action needs typed commands, previews, policy checks, idempotency, and reversible compensation.
- (idea by coach) Assume the monolith lasts ten years: enforce module fitness tests, local ownership, schema namespaces, and zero shared-domain dumping ground from day one.

- (direction by coach) Favor a commerce kernel plus replaceable capability modules, with strict public contracts, outbox events, and dependency fitness tests.
- (insight by coach) Product-versus-Offer, Order-versus-Fulfillment, and Payment-versus-Ledger are the three boundary splits that prevent single-store assumptions from infecting the platform.
- (insight by coach) The counterfactual delivery-of-value model unifies physical shipment, digital entitlement, service capacity, subscriptions, and bookings without forcing them into one fulfillment implementation.
- (insight by coach) Invariant ownership plus freshness and reconciliation contracts turns eventual consistency from an implementation accident into a governed domain decision.
- (insight by coach) Capability fractals and strangler trigger cards form one evolution system: every module has a maturity path and an evidence threshold for extraction.
